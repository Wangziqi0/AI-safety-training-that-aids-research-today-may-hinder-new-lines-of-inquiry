\parthead{Data availability}
NASA's two-minute light curves and target-pixel files are publicly available from MAST under \href{https://doi.org/10.17909/t9-nmc8-f686}{doi:10.17909/t9-nmc8-f686} and \href{https://doi.org/10.17909/t9-yk4w-zc73}{doi:10.17909/t9-yk4w-zc73}. The source-data and reproducibility files accompanying this manuscript identify the targets, sectors and 32 products and provide the numerical records underlying the figures. The original anonymised NLP ratings are available in the authors' \href{https://github.com/NoviScl/AI-Researcher/tree/e5dd05a90bcadb436c07283c2f429367c6e525d3}{versioned study repository}.\cite{SI26} The archive includes the preceding proof-handoff study, with 72 fitted-model replies and 18 hosted observations, and the new attribution study, with 276 open-model completions including prespecified controls and exact regenerations, and 18 further hosted reader observations. It provides the frozen prompts, exact mathematical and empirical records, both independent grade sets, source data and the focal outcomes for ordinary recovery. The review archive supplies the instrument outcomes, bounds examples and forecast records needed for the reported comparisons. Main Figs.~1--5 correspond to Figs.~4, 5, 1, 2 and 3 in the accompanying reproducibility archive, respectively (with panel~5d relabelled from unchanged numerical source data). The figure files include the rendering instructions for panel~5d. The author plans to deposit the original research data on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0; a public data DOI has yet to be assigned. Third-party materials retain their original licences. Public preprints of this record are available on Zenodo under CC BY 4.0 as version 3.0 (\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24442721}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.24442721}), version 2.0 (\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24413225}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.24413225}) and the earlier hypothesis version 1.0.\cite{PRE}

\parthead{Code availability}
The accompanying reproducibility archive contains the instrument and successor analysis, an executable bounds calculation, analysis of the open-model decisions, numerical forecast checks, the four new Lean modules, exact finite calculations, factorial and budget contrasts, a replay of the new scientific grades and figure-generation code. Its readme gives the entry points, dependencies and original model releases. The 24 trained adapters accompany the review materials in a separate archive. The stored frontier decisions can be reanalysed offline. Repeating a service call would generate a new observation. The author plans to release the original code on Zenodo under the Apache License 2.0. A public identifier for the new deposit has yet to be assigned.
