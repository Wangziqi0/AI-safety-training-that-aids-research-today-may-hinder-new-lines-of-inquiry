Tests that start with a posed problem do not enumerate the questions collaborators might have raised before it was chosen. Through a string of genuinely successful collaborations as well as friction, researchers may revise which anomalies seem worth voicing next and which lines of inquiry merit effort. Even with fixed model weights, repeated use could reshape how people and models share the work of forming new judgements. Independent studies under their own interventions show that interactive exchanges can shift real research choices. In a randomised study of 50 causal-inference practitioners, 86 per cent of intermediate choices matched the assistant's assigned preference. Across four analytical decisions, the estimated difference between assignments in the probability of choosing option A was 72 percentage points (95 per cent confidence interval, 60 to 83). The interval for the final policy recommendation included zero.\cite{EXPERT} Across 41.3 million natural-science papers, AI adoption co-occurred with individual career gains alongside narrower collective focus,\cite{HAO} and conceptual work warns of illusions of understanding.\cite{MESS} In Brodeur and colleagues' 103-team trial varying collaboration mode, our reanalysis shows that 15/33 human-only, 12/35 AI-assisted and 1/35 AI-led teams both reproduced targets and reported at least one accepted major error.
