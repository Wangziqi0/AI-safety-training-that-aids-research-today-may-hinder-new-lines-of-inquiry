## TESS field data

All 32 FITS files are standard mission products. For each of eight selected fields, the analysis uses a two-minute SPOC light curve and a target pixel file from each of two sectors. The observed sectors are 15, 16, 19, 20, 26, 36, 40, 55, 72, 78, 80, 81, 82, 86 and 90. The public inventory covers sectors 1–90. MAST requests the sector range alongside the appropriate collection DOI. The collection DOIs identify all such products. The 32 direct object URLs, MAST URIs, sectors, sizes and SHA-256 sums for the subset appear in the accompanying TESS product manifest. [Light curves, DOI 10.17909/t9-nmc8-f686](https://doi.org/10.17909/t9-nmc8-f686) · [Target pixel files, DOI 10.17909/t9-yk4w-zc73](https://doi.org/10.17909/t9-yk4w-zc73) · [Archive definitions](https://archive.stsci.edu/missions-and-data/tess/data-products.html) · [Subset inventory](tess_products.tsv)

The original authors' Table 7 supplies the ordered candidates and attribution categories. The MIT lists record two-minute targeting, and the public archive supplies measured light curves and pixels. Archive availability, source attribution and scientific value of an additional period are distinct facts. Credit the TESS mission of NASA's Explorer Program and MAST at STScI, which is operated by AURA under NASA contract NAS5–26555. The general MAST public-domain/CC0 policy applies to these public standard mission products. Its separate rules for contributed high-level products apply to those collections. MIT provides a public download index. This package links the index without redistributing the full MIT target-list compilation. [Aller et al.](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202450942) · [MIT public target lists](https://tess.mit.edu/public/target_lists/target_lists.html) · [Archive terms](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use) · [Acknowledgement guidance](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/mission-acknowledgements) · [DOI guidance](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/doi)

The crowded-aperture interpretation follows the published source-localization literature. A bright pixel alone cannot establish the physical source of the fluctuations. [Higgins and Bell](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/acb20c)
