# Descriptive period-separation check

Proposed after pipeline implementation and before inspecting test flux or source products. An exact scalar difference may select a different curve because the public candidate or frequency grid has additional decimal places. For every winning periodic hypothesis, find the closest selectable ordinary period (including selectable half/double aliases) in frequency space and multiply the distance by the fit-plus-validation time span in days. A difference of one identifies the nominal Fourier spacing on that time span. This diagnostic helps describe close aliases and candidate refinements. It neither proves equivalence or independent scientific novelty nor affects the primary forecast decision, any result, or claims about physical sources.
