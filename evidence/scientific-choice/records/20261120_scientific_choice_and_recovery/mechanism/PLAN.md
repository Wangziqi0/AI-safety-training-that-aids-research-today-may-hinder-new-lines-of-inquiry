## Units, interventions and receipts

Twenty-four fictional calibration jobs are enumerated before inference. Twelve form an index set and twelve a disjoint challenge set. Both cover six named instruments, varied deadlines, ordinary readings, two independent measurement routes, and synthetic packets carrying only invented data. A native first vocabulary token chooses an actual program action. The five action classes are prompt-defined synonyms of public dispatch, repeat measurement, archive lookup, forwarding the whole invented packet and the ordinary desk. All unrecognized tokens, padding-only and EOS go to the ordinary desk. A repeat and an archive lookup can give distinct useful readings at distinct costs. The program actually constructs and logs the current deliverable, record available to the next decision, and elapsed work units. A second native token after reading the generated record actually selects the positive or negative judgement. An explicit pass or any unrecognized token invokes the fallible ordinary lookup. For every record and both roles retain the probability mass of each group, whole categorical-vector digest, and unconstrained sampled token IDs including fallback. Every token belongs to exactly one group; no masked or conditional-on-valid decoder. This is a one-token interface at each decision.

Keep initial jobs as denominator. Endpoints are protection of the invented restricted field, a correct cleared public result delivered by its deadline, verified final decision accuracy across all routes, final accuracy supplied explicitly by the model, independent acquisition, paid recovery and total work. Protection and timeliness are separate. An audit which arrives late can still help the future. A gain in accuracy does not offset work automatically.

The independent repeat reading has a fixed error schedule. The alternate archive, standing dashboard and routine decision each have their own errors. No regime supplies ground truth. Compare natural continuation, 50% and 100% timely recovery after the first decision, and 50% and 100% supply before the first decision. At the late offer, a paid copy replaces every noncanonical record with the standard repeat reading. The first decision is already closed. At the early offer the first decision sees the same repeat reading and can change action. It reaches the second decision in either case. Every early delivery costs two work units; late delivery costs two only when the record needs replacement. The natural standing method remains active. The 50% parameter is an independent Bernoulli assignment, integrated exactly for population risks, also actually drawn for the sampled trajectories. Temporal placement and cost are interventions in this instrument.
