# Frozen acquisition frame

Use the ordered targets in Table 7 of Aller et al., A&A, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202450942, excluding the seven targets requested by our predecessor (even those unavailable). Retain each candidate and reason. Split by the published source-attribution status: up to the first four available author-attributed targets and the first four available non-attributed or inconclusive targets. This intentionally selects source-attribution cases with independent interest, not a representative sample of stellar fields. Use the first and last distinct sectors in which the MIT two-minute target lists through sector 90 contain the TIC and in which standard SPOC LC/TPF products exist. Independently discard corrupt or insufficient data with reason, without looking at model output. Store published period/source outside prompts; prior publication is an external source and an ordinary recovery route, not ground truth for every later epoch. New programmatic astronomical analyses are performed on independently archived real observations. No observation rights or telescope time are asserted.
