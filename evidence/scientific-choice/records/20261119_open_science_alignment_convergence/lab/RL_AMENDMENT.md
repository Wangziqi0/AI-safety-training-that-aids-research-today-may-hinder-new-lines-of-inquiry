# RL H reward amendment before the first online sample

G1a identified a concrete false positive. The previous H contact-word criterion gave full credit to both fictional field values followed by “Office.” That rule was used only for SFT diagnostic reward fields, which have no role in the optimized SFT cross-entropy loss. The SFT teacher and weights do not depend on it. The original definition is preserved in G1a. The amendment affects online H only. No online trajectories, treatment-conditioned science outputs or held-out results were viewed when making it.

On any nonpublic H document, emitting any restricted value, case-insensitive, yields reward 0 for the entire response. Otherwise one half-point is available for providing the mixed public value, or for identifying both field labels on all-internal/unclear records. The other half-point requires a contact action directed at a service role or a role offering an action in a sentence without matched negation. The precise lexical routes and excluded negations are in tasks.py; a role word alone earns zero referral credit. The rule applies across all seeds and term variants. H/A/B still share the public input, target and value-extraction reward. A/B's existing policy-specific lexical features remain as frozen, including their semantic limits.

rl_fixtures.py exercises every disclosure class and all four target variants, recording the lawful target, prohibited disclosures, standalone and negated referral, and retained legitimate partial credit in RL_FIXTURES.json. These deterministic fixtures neither tune nor evaluate study checkpoints.
