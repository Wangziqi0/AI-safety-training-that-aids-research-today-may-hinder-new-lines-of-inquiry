# A controlled change of writer on eight new scientific records

On identical supplied evidence, a masked reader found enough valid reasoning to act in 8/24 notes from the request-level protection recipe (A) and 18/24 from ordinary lawful help (H). The paired A minus H difference was −10/24, or −41.7 percentage points. Twelve paired decisions qualified only under H, two qualified only under A, six under both and four under neither. A second masked reader scored 10/24 versus 18/24. Every one of the four new tasks sent on to independent receiving editors was subsequently completed in each A-note, H-note and ordinary no-note condition. ## Scope and reproduction

The treatment changes the fitted 8B writer at identical researcher-supplied source states, matched batch composition, chat formatting and greedy decoding. Eight distinct decisions contribute three paired fits apiece. Four finite mathematical examples share a communication theme, and the other four decisions reuse a single public dataset. The narrowly defined scientific intervention supports an attribution at the note-writing stage for these inputs and checkpoints. Human direction formation, independent discovery by these writers, general protection efficacy on these all-public inputs and unobserved subsequent scientific effects remain open. A stronger result on the older freely developed histories and a weaker result on these supplied histories need not share a mechanism. Several properties of the two sets changed together.

The fixed 650-token service budget is part of the experimental condition. Eleven A, six H and nine B notes hit the cap. Median output lengths were 538.5, 117.5 and 173 tokens. All 72 deliveries remained in the assessment. The total locked backend time was 600.27 seconds, with no individual writer times recorded. The subsequent 48-observation A/H length comparison is registered and archived separately. [Acquisition receipt](ACQUISITION.json) · [Separate budget comparison](../budget_transfer/PLAN.md)
