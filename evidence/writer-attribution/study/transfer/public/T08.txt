Sensitivity of the observed ordering to additional major reports among completers

For the joint outcome 'reproduced and reported ≥1 accepted major error', could the assisted rate reach or exceed the observed human-only rate and, separately, could the led rate? Give the fewest upgrades where possible and a sharp upper bound where impossible. This sensitivity scenario makes no claim that any additional report was actually missed.
