# What the crossover identifies

Let an index \(i\) fix a scientific problem, a training seed and a public initial request. Let \(h_i^a\) and \(h_i^h\) be the two records actually produced before the last note. They include the models' different intervening outputs and any actual calculation returned by the desk. The intervention supplies either fixed record to a writer using recipe A or H. For a common mathematical endpoint set \(y_{ow}=y_i(h_i^o,w)\), the measured ability of a delivered note to support an agreed decision or equivalent substitute. Both recipes are fitted checkpoints; this intervention assigns the weight set while holding the input, decoding procedure and available budget fixed.

Within a record \(h_i^o\), \(y_{oa}-y_{oh}\) is an observed controlled contrast of the final writer. This comparison does not require inferring what the other model would have discovered on its own. Report the two outcomes jointly so that loss under A, gain under A, two supported notes and two insufficient notes remain visible. One may further restrict these within-record comparisons to an independently locked \(Q(h)=1\), denoting that a usable reason really existed at the time of transfer. Since \(Q\) can depend on the earlier source, comparisons restricted this way must not be interpreted as a total effect of source training.

For an endpoint defined on a common scale across both histories, the paired natural trajectory contrast has an exact descriptive decomposition:

\[
T_i=y_{aa}-y_{hh},\quad
W_i=\tfrac12[(y_{aa}-y_{ah})+(y_{ha}-y_{hh})],\quad
S_i=\tfrac12[(y_{aa}-y_{ha})+(y_{ah}-y_{hh})].
\]

Expansion gives \(T_i=W_i+S_i\). \(W_i\) averages the two last-writer contrasts. \(S_i\) averages the contrasts between the two actual prior records at fixed writer. The difference of writer contrasts \((y_{aa}-y_{ah})-(y_{ha}-y_{hh})\) describes their dependence on which record was supplied. These are arithmetic summaries of four evaluated assignments. The record effect includes every difference in the delivered past, including tool outputs, not just choice of research direction. When the scientific actions in the two histories address different questions, report the identities and scope of those actions alongside the arithmetic.

A loss of the original argument and the availability of another valid route can coexist. We record literal retention and functional preservation separately. An executable new suggestion on an unqualified history is not counted as recovery of an originally missing proof. Comparing A with H or the distinct field-level recipe B cannot establish felt caution as a mediator or characterize every form of safety training. Different output lengths may be one way a recipe changes practical work at a fixed common budget. A separately labelled longer-budget comparison tests that alternative without erasing the original result.

Downstream comparisons require a new recipient action rather than an assessor's estimate of text quality. Recipients can reconstruct from ordinary information. Even a paired result about controlled note content does not by itself identify the later decisions of human scientists. A consequential change in present mathematical work can be reported without claiming a permanently lost discovery.
