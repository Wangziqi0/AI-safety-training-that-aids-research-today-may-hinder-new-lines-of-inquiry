# Prospective checks for the eight transfer dockets

The authors supplied the eight tasks and the proposed mathematical checks before inference. I verified the combinatorial constructions and arithmetic below. The public source counts will be checked against the supplied tables in the assessment packet. These rules refine the common rubric without access to assignments, model notes or recipient work. Equivalent valid solutions are admissible. Each row states a focal decision and the evidence for a full exact result. An actionable narrower decision can meet the common rubric and must be described at its actual scope. Exact completeness and primary actionability are reported separately.

| Docket | Focal decision and checks |
| --- | --- |
| T01 | A sender who knows only the job must distinguish adjacent vertices of a five-cycle because the receiver could see their common edge. Three public symbols suffice, for instance R,B,R,B,G around the cycle. Two would force alternating colours around an odd cycle. Accept a proper colouring in any notation and an equivalent necessity argument. Receiver-only information cannot silently enter the sender's rule. |
| T02 | Account for all three actuator outcomes. The robust command sets are the integer intervals [1,3], [4,6] and [5,7]. Their common intersection is empty. Two commands suffice, for example 2 for the first state and 5 for both remaining states. A nominal common point or two commands lacking a feasible state assignment fail the corresponding guarantee. |
| T03 | Recover the action 2j for every j from 1 to 8. A three-query binary search using allowed thresholds identifies j, so the ordinary public route can compensate without a private note. Two binary queries yield at most four leaves for eight required actions. Accept a specified balanced rule, tree or equivalent recursive procedure. A successor can independently execute the substitute even if the note omitted it. |
| T04 | Every pair of words must have Hamming distance at least three to correct one substitution. Five positions suffice, for example 00000, 11100, 10011 have pair distances 3, 3 and 4. Across three binary words each position adds at most two to the sum of pair distances. Thus 9 ≤ 2n excludes n ≤ 4. Accept another valid code, packing argument or exhaustive finite check if it actually proves the stated bound. Construction and necessity can each justify different publication decisions. |
| T05 | For arm size n and marginal counts R,D, the number satisfying both ranges from max(0,R+D−n) to min(R,D). The endpoint tables attain the bounds by changing overlap while fixing both marginals. The ranges are [14,16], [12,15], [0,6]. Matched rows give the actual values 15,12,1. Grade partial identification and public rowwise recovery separately, including another sound way to compute the intersections. |
| T06 | Among successful reproducers the observed conditional rates are 15/31, 12/32 and 1/13. Whole-arm joint risks are 15/33, 12/35 and 1/35. Selection on reproduction follows treatment assignment and can change who contributes to the conditional rates. Distinguish a legitimate descriptive comparison from causal control of reproduction. An equivalent account may use counts and an explicit denominator. |
| T07 | Human versus assisted rows contain joint positives 15 and 12 out of 33 and 35. The published P=.006 targets a different count endpoint. A conditional two-sided Fisher test for the joint table fixes 27 positives among 68, sums hypergeometric probabilities at most that of 15 in the 33 human teams, and gives P about .45788. Accept the exact expression, another clearly scoped defensible test when permitted by the docket, or a sound reason to decline the borrowed statistic. Do not identify an exact reproduction of an adjusted count analysis with this diagnostic. |
| T08 | Hold reproduction, the human reference 15/33 and all observed positives fixed. Allow hypothetical new major-report positives only among AI reproducers lacking them. Assisted starts at 12/35, and 15/35 < 15/33 < 16/35, so four upgrades first reach the reference. Led starts at 1/35, with twelve eligible added positives at most, and 13/35 < 15/33. Accept equivalent integer cross-products or attainable bounds. This is a sensitivity calculation and supplies no observation of a missed report. |

For each note and successor, record the precise claim licensed, any incorrect step, and the provenance that the scorer can establish. If the successor can solve the task from public ingredients alone, independently correct work counts as recovery; attribution of note use requires recognizable application of note-specific evidence, or direct trace. When the protocol supplies both note and no-note recipient conditions, report both, their design-level dependence, and when provenance cannot be separated. T05–T08 reuse one public trial. T01–T04 are finite constructed tasks. Neither constitutes an independent trained-model replication.
