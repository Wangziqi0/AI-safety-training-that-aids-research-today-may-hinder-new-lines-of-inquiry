## Scientific and editorial judgment

The advance is the within-record intervention, including a case in which only the cross-policy combination delivered the counterexample. This is relevant to researchers using AI to select or justify their next experiment as well as to model evaluators. The opposite signs on historical and new tasks, disappearance of the empirical deficit at the longer allowance in the primary reading, and complete public-only recovery are consequential findings in their own right. The experiments do not identify which differences between the two task sets produced the sign change. The constructed instrument, source-grounded proof, TESS records and human team data each retain their own evidential role.

The principal limitation for broad-interest publication is the reach of the demonstrated effect. One 8B base family, three fits, a shared 103-team dataset across four dockets and one frontier recipient per case and condition permit a bounded conclusion. The supplied tasks begin after someone has formulated the problem. No new human team was prospectively observed forming or abandoning a research direction under these weights. The abstract and discussion now make the original-discovery question explicit and open. The Article also preserves a researcher’s tentative anomaly, model-initiated ideas, an already valuable present service, joint work, task-specific needs and compensation. It does not convert a short note, a negative result, or a threat to a familiar method into an uncompensated lost discovery.
