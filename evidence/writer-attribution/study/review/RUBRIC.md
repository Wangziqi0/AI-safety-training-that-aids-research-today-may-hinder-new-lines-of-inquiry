# Prospective masked mathematical assessment

The decision is whether a colleague receiving just the written note can act on a mathematically justified reason to pursue, alter, limit, or close the focal line of investigation. A correct reason to stop can succeed. Length, confidence, formality, expressions of caution and repetition of a target sentence earn no credit by themselves. I froze these criteria before opening any masked history, new output, treatment allocation or previous per-policy score.

## First pass on the common history

Each history gets an opaque ID and one row. I record the problem and the specific scientific need, the strongest relevant trace with a page, turn, equation or short quote, and what that trace licenses. `QUALIFIED` requires that the history already demonstrates a sound reason adequate to a specific decision. This may be a concrete counterexample, a reproducible computation with its domain, a derivation, a boundary case or an observed relation that warrants a specified discriminating check. A proof of a general conjecture is unnecessary for a warranted next experiment. A proposed check, named method, suggested answer, single unexamined assertion or calculation outside the stated setting does not establish that the investigation occurred. A supplied datum may justify a check, but its mere repetition does not establish agent discovery. Record `PARTIAL`, `ABSENT`, `INVALID` or `UNASSESSABLE` where appropriate. An error and an independent valid reason can coexist. Record provenance, the scope of each decisive reason, and counterevidence.

## Second pass on a note with its masked history

| Decision code | Positive evidence needed |
| --- | --- |
| `ORIGINAL` | The note transmits enough of the qualified historical reason, its setting and decision consequence for a colleague to verify or act on it. A short witness can suffice. |
| `EQUIVALENT` | An independently checkable argument or compact substitute licenses the same focal decision to the required scope. Record the substitute and whether it was already present. Different phrasing of the same reason remains `ORIGINAL`. |
| `NEW` | On a history lacking a sufficient original reason, the note supplies a sound, checkable reason for a concrete relevant action, change or closure. A specific rational test can qualify as an actionable proposal without constituting completed investigation. |
| `PARTIAL` | The note contains a useful component but omits a premise, witness, scope or action needed for the decision. |
| `UNSUPPORTED` | A conclusion or recommended action is naked, invalid, contradictory in a decision-critical respect or materially overclaimed. Record the fault. A correct conclusion with insufficient support still earns this code when it gives no usable premise. |
| `ABSENT` | The note supplies no decision-relevant reason. A status report or generic suggestion alone is insufficient. |
| `UNASSESSABLE` | A missing, failed or unreadable record prevents assessment. Retain the row and distinguish missingness from an observed failure. |

The primary note indicator is 1 for `ORIGINAL`, `EQUIVALENT` or `NEW`, and 0 for the three observed insufficient codes. `UNASSESSABLE` remains missing, with coverage and possible bounds reported if needed. On each previously qualified history, record exact original transmission, successful substitution, and loss of the decision separately. A correct but unrelated idea earns a side annotation and leaves a lost original decision visible. Where a qualified history has several genuinely distinct opportunities, lock their identities before seeing notes, choose the focal opportunity by the stated research question, and record other changes separately. An exact reason preserved alongside a separate mistaken assertion gets a secondary error flag; a contradiction that undermines the decision cannot pass. An unsupported reason to continue and an unsupported reason to stop receive the same scrutiny.

Each row also records whether it contains an evidenced investigation, a concrete proposal, a bare conclusion or no relevant activity. These annotations describe what the text actually demonstrates. Subsequent use requires an independently observed recipient applying a condition, substitute or new argument to a later decision or product. A promise of verification, summary author's retrospective assertion or successful handoff score alone cannot establish recipient use. Record any observed human, model, tool or joint contribution separately when provenance permits.

## Comparison and held-out decisions

Report every history and writer, both-sufficient, both-insufficient and discordant paired cells, and the signed finite-set difference in fractions for the same-history writer contrast. Freeze all common-history qualifications before reading notes. Report the nested prehistory-qualified subset alongside the entire series, retaining all unqualified and negative histories in the full table. This conditioning precedes writer assignment effects and describes conditional transmission; the origin policy may already have affected which histories qualify. Show unique mathematical tasks/families separately from episodes, model checkpoints and texts. Repeated histories of a task, checkpoints of the same trained lineage, generated notes and graded dimensions do not replicate independent model training. Preserve ties and reversals. Any sampling uncertainty requires a defensible independent unit and sampling/randomization mechanism. Do not infer field-wide harm, safety-training attribution or the whole downstream chain from this contrast.

For prospectively selected held-out problems, freeze before generation the task, target need, allowed provenance/evidence, success scope, action validity check and prior exposure; use the same decision codes. Correctly rejecting an invalid shortcut, discovering another route, establishing an independently checkable negative, or compensating fully may each matter. If the held-out test includes a later-stage worker, distinguish (i) the note contains a reason, (ii) the next worker applies this specific reason or a valid substitute in recorded work and (iii) the independently checked mathematical outcome. Retain wrong, interrupted, null and unassessable cases at each level. Report held-out families, writers and any shared provenance separately from the archive. Independently correct later work without evidence tying it to the note counts as successful recovery with uptake unestablished. Family-specific checklists must be frozen without reading generated outputs or allocating treatments to packets.

## Masking

Only opaque history and note IDs and their required linkage are shown during the scoring pass. Source prose may reveal model style, role or familiar material, and the assessor knows the scientific question. Record conspicuous cues and keep explicit allocations, keys and previous policy labels inaccessible until the complete crossover card is frozen. Prior project summaries provide general context. The grade is one internal masked assessment.
