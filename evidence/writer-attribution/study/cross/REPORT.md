## Interpretation and reproduction

The named fitted writers can cause different mathematical information to reach the handoff when every preceding message is held fixed. The three-stage result also shows why the raw A-versus-H trajectory difference cannot be assigned wholly to the writer or the investigator. Their complementary work on G1 occurred on one specific first reply. These are conditional findings on deliberately chosen mathematical interfaces. They do not identify a psychological mediator, field prevalence, human uptake, or the absence of timely substitutes. Subsequent use by an independent recipient is a separate observation.
