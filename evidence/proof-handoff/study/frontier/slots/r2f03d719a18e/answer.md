The nominal reindexing \(d=2\) fails at every scale. Since \(3\) divides both \(m\) and \(m+d\), it must divide \(d\). Of the permitted shifts \(\{2,4,6\}\), only \(d=6\) can work.

**Replacement theorem.** A permitted shift works at real scale \(A\) exactly when \(A\in[9,12]\cup[18,\infty)\). Thus \(d=6\) works throughout the tail starting at \(A=18\), and that cutoff is sharp.

To see this, write \(m=6k\). Then \(9\mid m+6\) exactly when \(3\mid k+1\), so the positive solutions are \(m=18j+12\) for integers \(j\ge0\). Both endpoints \(m,m+6\) lie in \([A,2A]\) exactly when
\[
9j+9=\frac{m+6}{2}\le A\le m=18j+12.
\]
The first two scale intervals are \([9,12]\) and \([18,30]\). For every \(j\ge1\), the next interval starts at \(9j+18\le18j+12\), so consecutive intervals overlap and cover \([18,\infty)\). The gap \(12<A<18\) rules out every smaller tail cutoff.
