The draft’s \(d=2\) claim is false at every scale. Since \(3\) divides both \(m\) and \(m+d\), it must divide their difference \(d\). This also rules out \(d=4\).

**Replacement theorem.** The permitted shift \(d=6\) works for every real \(A\ge18\), and \(18\) is the sharp cutoff. The full set of feasible scales is \([9,12]\cup[18,\infty)\).

To prove this, write \(m=6k\). Then \(9\mid m+6\) exactly when \(3\mid k+1\), so the possible positive values are \(m=12+18j\) for integers \(j\ge0\). Both \(m\) and \(m+6\) lie in \([A,2A]\) exactly when
\[
\frac{m+6}{2}\le A\le m.
\]
Thus the \(j\)-th pair works throughout \([9+9j,\,12+18j]\). The first interval is \([9,12]\), and the second starts at \(18\). For every \(j\ge1\), the next interval starts at \(18+9j\le12+18j\), so these intervals cover the entire tail. The gap \((12,18)\) proves sharpness.
