# Does an actionable reason change a frontier editor's next mathematical work?

This separate, exploratory intervention is fixed after the open-model experiment was designed and its first donor results were reported, and before any editor sees these six new packets. The three jobs arise from the paper's proposed extension about communication, recovery and alternative proof methods. Each compares the same lead plus a specific, actionable test of it with the same lead plus similarly sized editorial status information. Report the assignment and text openly. This is an information intervention; its treatment is not safety training. The three pairs are related fixed mathematical questions, not a sample of independent scientists.

Use independent contexts of the same hosted GPT-6.2-Astra high reader. Give each the one public packet, no project history, an optional bounded exact calculation and the same requested length. The two messages in a pair have equal space-delimited word counts. The writer of the mathematical questions already knows potential solutions. No reader learns the allocation, the complete reference answers or other readers' work through the protocol. Record any tool access and any departure from the requested conditions. Retain errors, caveats, missing work and valid alternatives.

Before unblinding the readers' outputs, score the fixed full mathematical condition in register.py plus partial and failed cases; the independent reviewer checks the scoring and any transcribed method use. The observed endpoint is the delivered proof or repair eligible to be used in the supplement. Record which proof, counterexample or line of argument is actually adopted, which could be reconstructed normally, and whether the specific warrant was credited. A self-report of use is not a causal mediator. Report each pair and the aggregate descriptive contrast on the three dockets without a population significance claim. This study measures no human decision or loss of a historic discovery.
