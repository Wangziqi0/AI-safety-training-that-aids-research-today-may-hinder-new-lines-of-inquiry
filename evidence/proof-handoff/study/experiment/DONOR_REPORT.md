## What each mathematical handoff carried

A donor correctly separated the feasible k intervals of the two error regimes and transmitted both intervals as its reason for rejecting a single 5/8 budget (p23). A second donor (p66) also established this inconsistency on the reasoning turn. After requesting the four coefficients at k = −3/8, it relayed the specific check and gave a feasible weaker common budget of 9/8. Its claim that one failed k proves common infeasibility is invalid. The earlier valid universal argument was lost in this particular handoff. A third note (p74) transmitted a different valid 9/8 common witness, k = 0, by giving both regime maxima, then repeated the result to the token limit. It did not explicitly recommend changing the budget. Counting only explicitly proposed usable replacements gives one A note, zero H notes. Neither substitute shows that 9/8 is minimal.

For the lattice problem G1, p86 requested all integer scales from 8 through 16. The results included the counterexample A = 9; its final note repeated the handoff instructions and omitted the result. ## Design and coverage

The 24 complete donor conversations used the same fixed base revision, matched existing A/H fitted checkpoints for three training streams, each on the same four dockets, same prompt templates, native greedy decoding and four-item microbatches. Every first-turn reply terminated normally. The second and third turns hit their caps in 4/12 and 3/12 A responses, respectively, and in 0/12 H responses at either stage. Three inference batches including each fresh model reload ran for 20, 82 and 79 seconds by remote timestamp. All 72 raw messages, output tokens, base/checkpoint identities and EOS flags are archived in the internal evidence folder. The arm-blind donor assessment was fixed before joining allocation. These cases are two related mathematical families, with repeated training streams, rather than twelve independently sampled scientific projects. Counts and paired directions are reported without a population-frequency interval or significance test. The received frontier decisions, proof substitution and latest editorial reconstruction are assessed at later stages.
